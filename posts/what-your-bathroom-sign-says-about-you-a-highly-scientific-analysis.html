What Your Bathroom Sign Says About You (A Highly Scientific Analysis)

Notes from the workshop · Bluegrass Gifts

There is a moment in every home tour — you know the one — where your host gestures vaguely down the hall and says 'bathroom's the second door on the left' and you shuffle off, and then you're standing in a stranger's most private room, and you have approximately ninety seconds to learn everything there is to know about this family's soul. The towels tell you something. The candle situation tells you something. But nothing, and I mean nothing, tells you more than whatever is hanging on that wall.

I have been in bathrooms with motivational quotes about hustle. I have been in bathrooms with framed cross-stitch that said things I cannot repeat here. I once used the facilities at a holiday party and discovered an entire gallery wall dedicated to vintage seed catalog illustrations, which honestly raised more questions than it answered. Every single one of those rooms told me exactly who I was dealing with before I had finished washing my hands.

Here is my unofficial taxonomy, developed over years of being a nosy houseguest.

The Nothing On The Walls bathroom belongs to someone who is either extremely zen or extremely busy, and there is no way to know which without further investigation. These people are unknowable. Approach with curiosity.

The Inspirational Quote bathroom — 'She Believed She Could So She Did,' that sort of thing — belongs to someone who is genuinely trying their best every single day, and honestly? Good for them. We should all be so earnest. The world would be a kinder place. I still feel a little haunted every time I see one, but that is my problem, not theirs.

The Nautical Themed bathroom belongs to someone who went on a cruise in 2009 and has never fully returned. There are rope accents. There is possibly a lighthouse figurine. The hand soap smells like the ocean, which the actual ocean does not, but we accept this.

Now. The Funny Sign bathroom. This is the one I want to talk about.

You know these signs. 'Wash Your Hands, Ya Filthy Animal.' 'If You Sprinkle When You Tinkle.' 'Please Remain Seated for the Entire Performance.' These households have made a choice, and that choice is: we are not taking ourselves seriously in here, and we are inviting you not to either. There is something deeply generous about that. You walk into a stranger's bathroom already a little vulnerable — it is an inherently awkward social transaction — and they have put a joke on the wall specifically so you can relax.

I grew up in a house where my grandmother had a small wooden sign in her hall bath that said 'Bless This Mess' and honestly that sign prepared me for adult life better than most of my formal education. It said: things are imperfect, we know it, we're fine. Come on in.

The best funny bathroom signs have a particular quality to them, which is that they feel like they were chosen by a specific human being with a specific sense of humor, not grabbed off a shelf because it was the first mildly amusing thing someone saw. There is a difference between a sign that makes you smile and a sign that makes you think oh, I know exactly the kind of person who hung this, and I like them already. That second kind is rarer. That second kind is the whole point.

If you are currently looking at your bathroom wall and it is either blank or holding something that no longer represents who you are — if the sea glass art was your ex's idea, if the motivational quote is starting to feel like a tiny judgment every morning — I would gently suggest that you think about what a joke you would actually tell. Not what looks nice. Not what matches the towels. What would make your specific brand of weird feel a little more like home.

Your bathroom is the one room in the house where every single guest ends up alone with your taste in humor for sixty uninterrupted seconds. Make it count. Make them laugh. Make them walk back out to the party thinking I really like these people.

That is, in my opinion, the whole point of a home.
