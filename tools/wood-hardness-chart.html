The Janka hardness chart below ranks 40 common woodworking species by the force (in pounds-force) needed to press a steel ball halfway into the wood — the standard measure of how well a wood resists dents and wear. Density and a working note ride alongside, so one table answers "how hard, how heavy, and what's it like to cut." Values are published averages at roughly 12% moisture content; individual boards vary.
